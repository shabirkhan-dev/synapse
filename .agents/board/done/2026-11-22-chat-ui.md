## What

Improve the chat UI (the human, 2026-11-22: "improve the chat ui").

## Resolution

Done. Commit: this change (`feat(console): calmer chat — centred thread, greeting, documents chip, grouped list (syn-092)`).

- **Layout:** the thread and its heading sit in one centred column (`max-w-3xl`). Replies use the full
  column; your messages are soft grey bubbles, without a border.
- **New chat:** a centred greeting ("What would you like to know?") with the product mark, three
  suggestion cards with icons, and the composer right under them (focused). Once the first message
  is sent the composer docks at the bottom. An error on the very first message shows in the greeting
  layout too; before, the error vanished in that layout, which was a bug.
- **Composer:**
  - A 16px card with the popup shadow and a soft blue focus ring.
  - Its toolbar holds the documents to search as a chip ("All documents", or the chosen one or the
    count). The chip opens a popover with the checkbox list and is named "All documents,
    documents to search in".
  - The hint sits under the box with `kbd` keys.
  - The Playground keeps the expandable "Search in" box; both share `DocumentScopeOptions`.
- **Streaming:** "Thinking…" shows three dots that pulse only when motion is allowed. A "Jump to
  latest" button appears above the composer when you have scrolled more than 240px up from the end.
- **Conversation list:** "New chat", a "Filter conversations" field (by title, in the page), and
  groups Today / Yesterday / Previous 7 days / Older (`utils/group-conversations.ts`, local days).
  The groups are labelled sections with mono capitals, not headings, because the page heading comes
  after the list. Rows are one line with the date in the tooltip; delete stays on hover or focus.

Validation:
- Console: 121 pass, including `group-conversations.test.ts`, the chip in the chat scope test, and
  the refused first message.
- e2e: chat, chat-history, answer-flow, onboarding, layout, search-and-loading and accessibility
  (axe in light and dark) pass.
- Screenshots: new chat, thread, the popover, and a phone view.
